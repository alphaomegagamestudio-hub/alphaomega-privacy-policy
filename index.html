1. Introduction

AlphaOmegaGameStudios (“we,” “our,” or “us”) develops and publishes mobile games and applications, including games available through Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our games and applications (“Apps”).

Our portfolio may include games designed for children aged 3 and older, older children and teenagers, and adults. Each App has its own intended audience, as specified in its Google Play listing. We handle information and configure advertising and analytics services according to the App's audience, applicable privacy laws, and Google Play policies.

Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to review this policy before allowing children to use our Apps.

2. Information We May Collect

The information processed depends on the particular App, its features, and its integrated services.

Technical information

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • App version, language, and technical settings.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics, and performance information.

App usage information

  • Gameplay events and progress.
  • Session duration and general interaction statistics.
  • Information used to understand app stability and improve gameplay.

Advertising and network information

Where AdMob is enabled, advertising service providers may process information needed to deliver, protect, and measure advertisements, subject to applicable advertising and privacy requirements. Service providers may also process technical network information, such as IP addresses, for service delivery, security, and other disclosed purposes.

We do not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or telephone number merely to play our games unless a particular feature expressly requires it.

Our child-directed Apps are not intended to collect precise GPS location, contact lists, photographs, or other sensitive personal information. The exact information processed by third-party services depends on the SDK versions and configurations used in each App.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be processed to operate and maintain our Apps, provide gameplay functionality, diagnose crashes and technical problems, improve stability and user experience, understand general gameplay patterns, deliver and measure advertisements where enabled, and detect abuse, fraud, and security issues.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information to build behavioral advertising profiles or target children with personalized advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

Some Apps are designed for children aged 3 and older. We recognize that children require additional privacy protection.

We seek to comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where applicable, and Google Play's Families Policy. For child-directed Apps, we configure data collection, advertising, and integrated SDKs to meet the requirements applicable to those Apps.

We do not knowingly request children's personal contact information for ordinary gameplay. Where collection, use, or disclosure of a child's personal information requires verifiable parental consent or another legally required authorization, we will obtain the necessary authorization before proceeding.

Parents and legal guardians

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to ask what personal information relating to their child may be processed, request access to or deletion of their child's personal information where applicable, request that further collection or use stop, or raise concerns about advertisements, analytics, or third-party services.

We will review these requests and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and the capabilities of relevant service providers.

5. Games for Teenagers and Adults

Some Apps may be designed for teenagers aged 13 and older or for adult audiences. The intended audience is declared separately for each App in Google Play Console. Privacy practices applicable to an App depend on its actual audience and features.

An age of 13 does not automatically mean that a user is considered an adult under every country's laws. Certain laws and Google Play requirements may continue to protect teenagers and other users under the applicable age of majority.

We apply appropriate privacy and advertising safeguards according to the App's target audience and applicable requirements.

If an App is designed for both children and older audiences, we implement applicable age-screening and advertising safeguards and restrict advertising and data collection for children and users whose age is unknown, as required.

6. Advertising — Google AdMob

Some Apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Advertising practices depend on the target audience declared for the App.

Child-directed Apps

For Apps targeting children, or serving children and users of unknown age as applicable, we configure advertising requests and ad services to meet Google Play Families requirements. Safeguards include no personalized advertising or remarketing directed at children, appropriate child-directed advertising treatment, age-appropriate ad content and formats, eligible Families-compliant advertising SDKs and mediation sources where required, and restrictions on advertising identifiers and other information where required.

For child-directed advertising requests, we apply the applicable maximum ad content rating and other required safeguards.

Teen and adult Apps

For Apps designed for older audiences, advertising is configured according to the declared audience, applicable law, and relevant advertising policies. Personalized advertising is not enabled for users where prohibited or where necessary legal requirements have not been met.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

7. Analytics — Firebase Analytics

Some Apps use Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay activity, app usage, stability, and performance. Depending on the products and settings enabled, Firebase may process app events, session information, device-related information, and technical diagnostics.

We configure Firebase Analytics according to each App's intended audience and applicable requirements. Collection and transmission that are not permitted for child-directed Apps are disabled or restricted as necessary. We do not knowingly use Firebase Analytics to create behavioral advertising profiles for children.

Firebase Privacy Information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Firebase collection, processing, and retention depend on the products enabled, their settings, and the relevant service terms.

8. Other Third-Party Services

Depending on an App's functionality, it may use Google Play Services or other third-party services for platform features, achievements, leaderboards, diagnostics, or other functions.

We review integrated SDKs and services for suitability and compliance with the App's intended audience and applicable requirements. Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and service terms. We remain responsible for configuring and disclosing integrations appropriately.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell children's personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to support app functionality, advertising, analytics, security, and diagnostics, subject to applicable law and the safeguards described in this policy.

We do not knowingly permit children's personal information to be used for personalized advertising or remarketing directed at children. Any additional sharing will be handled according to the relevant App's functionality, applicable law, and disclosed data practices.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal obligations and the retention practices of our service providers.

Parents, legal guardians, and other users may contact us to request deletion of personal information where applicable. We will review requests and take appropriate action, including contacting relevant service providers where necessary.

Uninstalling an App may stop future collection by that App, but it does not necessarily delete information already retained by third-party services.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed through our Apps against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our service providers may also implement security measures for information they process. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence. Such processing is subject to applicable legal requirements and relevant safeguards offered by those providers.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Apps, services, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage users and parents or legal guardians to review the policy periodically.
